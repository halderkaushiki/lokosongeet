Meet Kaushiki Halder

Building accessible digital tools for preserving folk culture and connecting communities.

KH

Lokosongeet is a beginning.

This project brings together design, technology, and cultural curiosity to make folk and folk-related oral and performance traditions easier to discover, study, and care for.

The archive is being developed with respect for performers, communities, provenance, consent, and the difference between a sample interface and verified cultural documentation.
